## Prompt della tavola di riferimento

Create a finished character lineup illustration for Mana Arena, an Italian Magic tournament website. Five DISTINCT adorable penguin mascots, full body, all in one wide horizontal row with generous transparent gaps, same ground line, no overlapping silhouettes, facing front / gentle three-quarter. Soft polished 3D cartoon collectible-toy aesthetic, rounded tactile feather/fur surfaces, expressive eyes, short orange beaks and feet, consistent art direction and warm studio light, charming not aggressive. Penguin anatomy recognizably penguin with flippers. They must NOT be the same model recolored: distinct builds, faces, tufts, poses and fantasy archetypes. Left to right exactly: WHITE angel with wings and gold halo; BLUE illusionist with cobalt wizard hat and aqua bubble; BLACK friendly vampire with amethyst cape and tiny fangs; RED stocky pyromancer with flame crest and artificer goggles; GREEN broad round druid with twig antlers and sprouting wooden staff. Green wears NO cape, NO cloak, NO mantle, NO leaf clothing: natural fluffy black-and-white torso plainly visible. Original characters, no official logos/mana symbols, text or watermark. Actual transparent alpha background suitable against dark navy.
